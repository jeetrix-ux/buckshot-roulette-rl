Buckshot Bot Lab

How our bot learned to play. A hand-built decision ladder, sitting on an exact model of how the gun is loaded, gets its 39 weights tuned by the cross-entropy method. It trains in a league of baseline bots and frozen copies of itself. Scrub through the generations, then step through real games and see why each decision was made.

Why it can win

The gun is not shuffled fairly

The engine loads the gun by flipping a fair coin for each slot while both shell types remain, and then fills the rest with what's left. So the next shell is a 50/50 whenever both types are still in the gun, whatever the count says. It only becomes certain once one type runs out. Most bots assume the chance is lives ÷ shells. Is it getting better?

Fitness is placement points averaged over mixed 2, 3 and 4-player tables, scaled so a win is +1 and last place is −1. The league gets harder as frozen copies of the bot join it, so the training score levels off while the real skill keeps rising.
